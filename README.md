Marine Pollution Analysis — Bay of Algiers

Overview

This project investigates marine environmental conditions in the Bay of Algiers through a coupled analysis of in-situ physico-chemical measurements and Sentinel-2 satellite remote sensing.

The objective is to assess whether satellite-derived spectral information can support the spatial characterization of marine water-quality-related parameters.

Objectives

- Analyze measured physico-chemical and environmental parameters.
- Process Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery.
- Evaluate spectral indices related to water properties.
- Investigate relationships between satellite-derived indices and field observations.
- Assess the ability of remote sensing to support spatial marine environmental mapping.
- Produce spatial representations of environmental conditions across the study area.

Study Area

The study focuses on a coastal site within the Bay of Algiers, Algeria.

Field observations were collected during March, April and May 2025.

The study combines field measurements with Sentinel-2 imagery to investigate spatial and temporal variability in marine environmental conditions.

Field Data

The analysis considers several marine environmental parameters, including:

- Temperature
- Salinity
- pH
- Dissolved oxygen
- Turbidity
- Chlorophyll-a
- Suspended matter
- Nutrients

The field dataset is used to evaluate relationships between measured environmental conditions and satellite-derived information.

Remote Sensing

The project uses Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery.

Processing includes:

- Atmospheric correction
- Band extraction
- Spectral index calculation
- Spatial masking
- Monthly image compositing
- Statistical comparison with field observations

Spectral Indices

The analysis includes indices such as:

- NDTI
- NDCI
- CDOM-related indices
- FAI
- NDWI
- MNDWI

These indices are investigated as indicators of spatial variability in water properties.

Statistical Analysis

The relationships between field observations and satellite-derived variables are evaluated using statistical metrics including:

- R²
- RMSE
- MAE

Several modelling approaches can be evaluated to investigate the form and strength of relationships between observations and spectral variables.

Interpretation

The project distinguishes between spatial mapping potential and quantitative concentration estimation.

Weak statistical relationships do not necessarily prevent satellite-derived indices from being useful for identifying and mapping spatial patterns such as turbidity, suspended matter or chlorophyll-related variability.

Therefore, the results are interpreted with respect to the limitations of the available field observations, satellite resolution and temporal matching.
